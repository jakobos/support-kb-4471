The queued parser fix does not fix the other problems. Keep #4471's reports for the other problems open until their own tickets are fixed.

How each report is placed

  1. One report per id. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. They are counted once.
  2. Tenant, not browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), not on intake’s tag, which is a guess from the reporter’s browser.
  3. What moved. When day and month trade places, the cause is the shared parser, or the booking-v3 form hint for reports sent through that form. When the date moves by one day, no parser or form hint explains it: the desk’s one-day-move list (desk-notes.json) names those reports, and they get their own problem here instead of a footnote under parser.
